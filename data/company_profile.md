## 📜 3. Business Description & Value Proposition

### About the Company
Global Mediquips was incorporated in 2013 by **Shivashankar Katari** with a mission to deliver high-precision medical equipment and healthcare devices across India. Operating as a Sole Proprietorship in Amberpet, Hyderabad, the company balances direct hospital equipment manufacturing with wholesale distribution and retail supply.

### Product Portfolio Overview
Their wide range encompasses critical care and diagnostic equipment:
* **Respiratory Care**: BiPAP, CPAP, Oxygen Concentrators, Nebulisers.
* **ICU & Diagnostic**: Patient Monitors, 12-Channel ECG Machines, Syringe Pumps, Defibrillators, Vein Viewers.
* **Hospital Infrastructure**: 3-Function Motorized Beds, Examination Couches, Hydraulic Gynec Tables, Recovery Trolleys, Fowler Beds.
* **General Surgicals & Homecare**: BP Monitors, Glucometers, Thermometers, Orthopedic Supports, Surgical Wear, Medical Diapers, Weighing Scales, Cholesterol Meters, Body Fat Monitors.
